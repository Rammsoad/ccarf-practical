Review sheet: what would you do?

The review tables from the five guides, written as short exam situations. Read the situation, answer out loud and then look at the answer. The Typical distractor column is the option that sounds right but is wrong. To practice, cover the answers and click (or press Enter) on one to see it. It can be printed.
