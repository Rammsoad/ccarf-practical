╭───────────────────────────────────────────── 💡 Exam concept ──────────────────────────────────────────────╮
│                                                                                                            │
│  In long conversations the history gets compressed with summaries… and summaries lose what's exact:        │
│  amounts, dates, order numbers, what the customer already declined.                                        │
│  Pattern: a 'case facts' block with the transactional data that's copied in full in every turn.            │
│  Also: trim tool results to the relevant fields, and put what matters at the start or the end (the model   │
│  pays less attention to what's in the middle: 'lost in the middle').                                       │
│                                                                                                            │
╰────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╯
